Detail brush

Each click adds an area: "more detail" keeps faces (muzzle, paws), "less detail" removes them (back, flanks). The total number of faces stays as set.

Sheet and cutting

20°

0° = every face is a separate piece. Higher = larger sections to bend and fewer welds.

Hand bending

Each bend is engraved M (ridge up) or V (valley, down) with the angle to reach, seen from the side that comes out of the laser. Shorter bridges bend more easily but hold less: try them on an offcut of your thickness.

Quick assembly

The tab sticks out from the edge of one piece and is bent inwards, under the neighbouring piece: it holds it in place while you tack and backs the weld. Placed on convex edges only.
